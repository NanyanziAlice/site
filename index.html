Modeling adversarial attacks and defensive strategies for critical infrastructure resilience.

I am a PhD candidate in Industrial and Systems Engineering at the University of Oklahoma, advised by Kash Barker and Sridhar Radhakrishnan. My research models adversarial attacks and defensive strategies for critical infrastructure resilience, with a focus on how adversarially induced changes in human behavior affect the performance of interdependent systems.

About

I am a doctoral researcher in the School of Industrial and Systems Engineering at the University of Oklahoma. My work brings together network modeling, infrastructure resilience, and adversarial behavior. Before OU, I studied mathematics and mathematical sciences in South Africa and computer engineering in Uganda.

I also work as a Graduate Research Assistant, modeling false-claim attacks on critical infrastructure and designing protection, interdiction, and restoration strategies. I welcome conversations about research and collaboration.
